# Archive Clients to preserve past work

Ordinary Client removal will archive the Client instead of permanently deleting it and its related Enrollments, Issues, and Appeals. The current hard-delete behavior would make past work impossible to track and would conflict with restorable Documents, so Phase 3 changes that lifecycle rather than only blocking deletion for Clients with Documents. Restoration must preserve the Client's related records and their existing states.

Archiving removes the Client from ordinary active Client lists but does not hide or cancel open Tasks and scheduled Appointments linked to that Client. Those items remain visible with an archived-Client indicator so a Broker does not lose a deadline merely by archiving its Client.
